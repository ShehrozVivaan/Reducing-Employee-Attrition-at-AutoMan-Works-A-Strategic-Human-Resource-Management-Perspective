# Reducing-Employee-Attrition-at-AutoMan-Works-A-Strategic-Human-Resource-Management-Perspective
###This project focuses on reducing high employee attrition at AutoMan Works, a mid-sized automobile component manufacturing company. The case identified major HR problems such as high employee turnover, poor communication, lack of training, unfair compensation, limited career growth, and transportation difficulties for employees working at Unit 2.

###We analysed these issues using key Strategic HRM frameworks, including the Harvard Model, Best-Fit Approach, AMO Model, and Resource-Based View (RBV). Based on the analysis, we proposed solutions such as company transport/travel reimbursement, structured recruitment and onboarding, continuous training and mentorship, performance-linked pay, employee feedback systems, and digital shift communication.

###The main objective was to help AutoMan Works reduce employee turnover, improve employee satisfaction and engagement, retain skilled employees, and build a more stable workforce through strategic HR practices.
